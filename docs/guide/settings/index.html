Settings and tools

What you use, Off while away, Ctrl+Z, how OGP looks, and the ways it explains itself.

📄️Features

Choose what you use - each feature switch shows or hides its page. "What are you here for?" sets them in one go.

📄️Off while away

While VRChat says you're AFK, everything is off - and it all comes back by itself when you're back.

📄️Undo and redo

Ctrl+Z takes back your last changes anywhere in OGP; Ctrl+Y steps forward again.

📄️Appearance

Turn OGP from green to any colour, in a Vibrant or a Darker look. Both change while you watch.

📄️Help and the guided tour

How OGP explains itself - hover explanations, Show me around (F1), the Help page and the guided tour.
