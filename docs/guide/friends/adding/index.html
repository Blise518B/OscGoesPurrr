Adding friends

Friends link two OGPs over the internet, so each can drive the other's toys, shockers and avatar, as far as the other allows. There's no account and no server of ours: the two PCs talk directly, with free relays as a fallback when a direct line isn't possible.

Friends is on by default (Settings → Features → Friends — control each other over the internet). Nothing goes online until you pick a nickname.

The Friends page: your nickname and Online at the top, the friend list, and the picked friend's panel below.

1. Pick a nickname​

Type it into the box at the top. That's the name your friends see. Then OGP goes online, and the Online switch beside it is remembered from then on. Switch it off to go offline.

2. Send an invite link​

  1. Press ＋ Add a friend (they need OGP too). It opens by itself while you have no friend yet.
  2. Under SEND AN INVITE LINK, pick how long it works:
    • Once: the first friend who clicks it, within a day;
    • 1 day / 1 week: anyone who clicks it in that time;
    • No end: anyone who clicks it, until you close it.
  3. Press Copy invite link and paste it into Discord. It's a link that works in a browser too, with "(Add your name on OscGoesPurrr)" under it.

Your friend clicks it, OGP opens on their PC, and you're friends.

Or swap IDs​

Under the link is your ID, with Copy. A friend pastes it under GOT THEIR LINK OR ID? and presses Add; you press Accept when they ask. Your ID stays the same every time you open OGP. It's not a password: someone who has it can only ask to be your friend.

Close your links​

⋯ next to Copy invite link:

  • Close all invite links: every link you sent stops working. Your friends stay.
  • New ID - unfriend everyone…: for a link that got out of hand. Every friend is unlinked, and every link and your old ID stop working.

The friend list​

The list: you first, then your friends with their ping (Rin is offline, with ↻), your VRChat instance, and your groups.

  • You are always first: the Me row, "this PC". See Groups and Me.
  • Each friend shows their ping while linked, or offline.
  • The three columns are what each friend may use: toys, shockers, your avatar. See What each friend may do.
  • Click a friend to open their panel below. See Driving a friend.

Offline? Check now​

OGP looks for offline friends in the background, gently, so the link stays light. To look right away:

  • ↻ on an offline friend's row looks for that friend now;
  • ↻ Check all next to FRIENDS looks for everyone who shows offline.

The row says checking… until they answer or the try gives up. Each friend is looked for at most once every 10 seconds, however often you click.

Versions​

Each OGP tells its friends its version. The first time a friend's is newer than yours, OGP says how to update, and an amber banner stays on the Friends page. A friend on an older version gets an OLD tag on their row.

Unfriend​

At the bottom of a friend's panel: Unfriend name…. The other side drops you too.

note

One OGP per PC goes online. A second OGP window on the same PC stays offline.
