bHaptics

OGP drives a bHaptics suit, sleeves, hands, feet or head straight over Bluetooth: no bHaptics Player, no pairing in Windows. It plays your avatar's bHaptics contacts, and OWO's too if you like.

Switch it on first: Settings → Features → bHaptics — suit and sleeves over Bluetooth, or tick A bHaptics suit or sleeves in What are you here for?. It's off by default. Its page is Suits, shared with OWO and the haptic trackers.

The Suits page: front and back, every bHaptics motor an orange dot, OWO's muscles purple patches, and a haptic tracker as a blue puck.

The body​

The Suits page is a body, front and back. Every bHaptics motor is an orange dot; OWO's muscles are purple patches around them; haptic trackers are blue pucks.

  • Click a part of her to set it up: its card opens beside her, with a line to it. A click on empty space closes it.
  • Press and hold a dot to feel it.
  • Each card says whether the avatar you wear has that part's contacts. A dashed amber ring marks what's missing.
  • Touches light up as they land.

Connect​

  1. Close the bHaptics Player. A suit takes one connection at a time.
  2. Switch the suit on.
  3. Press Connect on the bHaptics card. Connect when the app starts does it every time from then on.

Let go hands the suit back to the Player or a game.

What you have​

You have lists the bHaptics parts: tick the ones you own. Only those show on her. A vest is ticked by default, and a part that connects ticks itself.

Your avatar's contacts​

All of these work, with no setup:

  • the official bHaptics avatar package (bOSC/v2…);
  • older bHapticsOSC setups;
  • with Also feel OWO's contacts on (the default), the OWO avatar package's contacts play the bHaptics motors around them.

Zone routes send an SPS zone or one of your SPS Sources to a part of the body, for both suits: Add route, then its zone, type and gain.

Safety​

  • If OGP stops sending, the suit goes quiet within a quarter of a second.
  • Off, VRChat going silent, Off while away and the suit's own anti-stuck (its Anti-stuck card in the bHaptics frame, with its own times) hold the suit too. The OWO suit has its own in the OWO frame.
  • Tell VRChat sends OGP/BHapticsConnected, and one per part. See "It's connected" parameters.
