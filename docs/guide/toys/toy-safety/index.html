Anti-stuck and Sleep

VRChat only sends a value when it changes. If a contact stops updating (you swap avatar, your partner leaves, the link drops), its last value would keep a toy, a suit or the headset running. Anti-stuck notices a value that has frozen and stops it. It's on by default.

Each kind of device has its own anti-stuck, with its own switch and times, on its own page, because they may want different settings:

DeviceWhere
Your toysThe Toys page, under Toy server. Each toy's bar has its own switch too.
The bHaptics suitThe Suits page, the bHaptics frame's Anti-stuck card.
The OWO suitThe Suits page, the OWO frame's Anti-stuck card.
Haptic trackersThe Suits page, under her, after the trackers.
The PSVR2 headsetThe PSVR2 page, at the bottom of the Contact card.

The Toys page: anti-stuck for every toy, under Toy server.

The two times​

A real contact between zero and one never holds perfectly still: animation and IK jitter it all the time. So:

  • Frozen part-way: stop after (1 s by default): a value that stops moving somewhere in the middle is cut quickly.
  • Held at 100%: ease off after (10 s by default): all the way in and held is a real state, so it gets the longer wait, then eases off over 3 s.

A new value brings it back at once. The times are sliders, from 1 s to 10 minutes; they hide while the switch is off.

Each suit has its own, in its frame on the Suits page.

If you updated from an older OGP, each one starts with the switch and times you had: before, one anti-stuck in Settings covered them all.

One toy: Sleep and Anti-stuck​

Each toy's bar, on Home and on the Toys page, has two chips beside Touch and Penetration:

  • Sleep puts just this toy to sleep: nothing from your avatar's contacts plays on it until three full strokes land within six seconds, so a brush against a sleeping partner does nothing. After 45 seconds without a stroke it sleeps again. While the sidebar's Sleep is on, every toy sleeps and every chip is lit (and greyed). Like the sidebar's, it isn't remembered when you restart OGP. Friends' drives still reach a sleeping toy.
  • Anti-stuck is on for every toy by default. Switch it off for a toy that should keep playing a value that sits still. It only counts while the Toys page's anti-stuck is on (greyed while that is off), and it's remembered, the same in every mode.

A toy's row: the Touch and Penetration chips, then Sleep over Anti-stuck.

Ctrl+Z asks first before it would switch an anti-stuck off or make it wait longer, as doing it by hand would.

Settings → Safety​

Settings' Safety card (called Toy Safety before) holds Off while you're away: while VRChat says you're AFK, everything is off. See Off while away. Above it, a button for each page with an anti-stuck takes you there.

Settings → Safety: buttons to each device's anti-stuck, and Off while you're away.

Other things that stop your toys​

  • Off in the sidebar, or OGP/Off from your avatar's menu.
  • Mute on a toy's row silences that one toy.
  • OGP's Test pulses (Test all, a toy's Test, OGP/Test) are gentle: about 20 % for a moment.
