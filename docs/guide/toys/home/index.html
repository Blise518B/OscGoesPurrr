Home

Home is where OGP opens: a row for everything you have, with its quick switches. The details live on each device's own page. Click a row to open it there.

Home's Devices: two connected toys, the shocker hub, the bHaptics and OWO suits, the PSVR2 headset, and a toy that's switched off, greyed at the bottom.

Getting started​

Until the basics work, the top of Home shows Toy on · VRChat connected · Avatar has SPS · Avatar menu, a dot each. It goes away once the first three are green. Don't show again folds it to one slim line for good, and Take the tour starts the guided tour.

Devices​

Test all buzzes every connected toy gently for about a second.

A toy's row​

Every toy you've connected gets a row, outlined in its own colour and numbered in the corner. The number is its place: toys keep the order they connected in, and friends see them in that order too.

From left to right:

  • Its name and a dot: green while connected. A toy you've used before but that's switched off stays listed, greyed, with OFFLINE.
  • What drives it in the active mode: All SPS, a zone's name, or how many zones.
  • The four chips: Touch from Self / Others, Penetration from Self / Others. A chip sets that choice on every chain of the toy, in the active mode. It shows half lit while the toy's chains disagree; a click makes them all agree. A chip greys out when none of the toy's chains hears that kind.
  • Sleep over Anti-stuck: Sleep puts just this toy to sleep (three full strokes wake it); Anti-stuck, on by default, can leave this one toy out. See Anti-stuck and Sleep.
  • Battery, and a live meter per motor.
  • Mute silences this one toy. Test buzzes it.

In a wide window it's all one line: the name, then the chips as two rows, Touch above Penetration with Sleep over Anti-stuck beside them, then the battery, meters, Mute and Test. Only when the window gets narrower do the zones and chips move to a line under the name, and in a narrow one the battery and meters get a line of their own too.

The shocker hub's row​

Arm shocks, Share with friends and On, with red words that say Shocks armed or Shocks not armed. These are the same switches as on the Shockers page: flip one and the other follows. Arming asks first. See Arming and limits. In a narrow window Arm shocks and Share with friends move to a line under the name; On stays at the end.

The shocker hub's row: PiShock with two shockers, armed, shared with friends, and on.

Suits and the headset​

The bHaptics and OWO suits each have On and Also feel OWO's contacts / Also feel bHaptics' contacts. The PSVR2 headset has On. See Suits and trackers and PSVR2.

The sidebar​

The sidebar shows the same things on every page: the four mode buttons, Total output strength, Off and Sleep, then the pages, then whether VRChat and Intiface are connected. See Modes, strength, Off and Sleep.
