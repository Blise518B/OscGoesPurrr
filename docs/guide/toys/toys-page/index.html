The Toys page

Home shows each toy at a glance. The Toys page, right under Friends in the sidebar, holds everything about them: each toy's signal chains, the toy server, SPS Sources and the tuning tools.

The Toys page: Scan now, the Toy server line, anti-stuck, and a card per toy. SPS Sources and Tuning tools are folded at the bottom.

Scan now​

OGP looks for toys by itself: every few seconds while no toy is connected, every 30 seconds while one is. Scan now looks right away, for a toy you just switched on. Beside it, OGP says when it looks next.

Toy server​

Whether the toy server is CONNECTED and where it runs: built in, on this PC, or your own Intiface Central. Intiface settings opens Settings → Intiface Engine. See Where your toys connect.

Anti-stuck​

Under Toy server: anti-stuck for every toy, with its two times. It stops a toy whose contact has frozen. Each toy's bar has its own Anti-stuck chip to leave one toy out. See Anti-stuck and Sleep.

A toy's card​

A card's bar is the same as the toy's row on Home: the four chips, Sleep and Anti-stuck, battery, meters, Mute and Test. Under it, the card is already open on each motor's chains, each folded to one bar: its name, a letter per stage lit by its live level, and its settings at a glance. Click a chain to open it. A click on the toy's bar folds the whole card away, and another brings it back.

A toy's chain opened: Chain 1 of its motor, from Input through Depth, Speed and Punch, Combine, Wake and Envelope to Zero cut and Output.

Signal chains​

Each motor has a signal chain: the stages that turn your avatar's contacts into the motor's level. Click a stage to edit it. Rest the mouse on a stage to see what each knob does.

StageWhat it does
InputWhere the signal comes from: which zones, touch or penetration, from you or from others.
DepthHow deep or close the contact is: the steady part of the feel.
SpeedHow fast the contact moves: the lively part. It fades once movement stops.
PunchA short hit on top of a fast thrust in (or pull out), then it decays.
CombineHow Depth and Speed merge: Max (the louder wins, the default), Add or Multiply.
WakeNothing plays until there's real contact. Strokes mode waits for full strokes, which is what Sleep uses.
EnvelopeHow the level moves over time: smoothing, and an optional texture so a steady hold feels alive.
Zero cutSnaps to silence the moment the contact is gone, instead of fading out. Off by default.
OutputA gain to balance this toy against the others, and the range the toy can use.

A motor can have up to six chains. + Add chain adds one that runs on the same input. A chain can be for Penetration, Touch or Custom; a coloured bar on its fold row says which.

Reset to defaults puts a chain's feel back to OGP's own.

Its zones​

  1. Open its chain, then click Input.
  2. Click Select Zones. These are your SPS sources: All SPS hears every SPS zone your avatar has. Or switch on only the ones you want: the zones VRChat reports for the avatar you wear, and the ones you built under SPS Sources.
  3. Who it hears: Touch from yourself, Touch from others, Penetration from yourself, Penetration from others. They're the same four as the chips on the toy's bar.

The zones are kept per mode. The feel isn't: you tune it once.

Your own parameters​

+ Add Variable, in a chain's Input stage, maps any OSC parameter to the motor. The motor follows the loudest of them, plus its zones.

The picker hides what's rarely wanted: VRChat's own parameters, GoGo Loco, face tracking, VRCFury's internals, physics assets, PhysBone states, OGP's own and SPS's. Each group is a chip with its count at the top of the picker. A lit chip hides its group, and Show everything shows them all. A search also finds hidden ones, under Hidden by filters. A parameter you already mapped is never hidden.

At the bottom of the card​

One small line, after every motor's chains:

  • Avatar parameters is folded, and says how many are on. Open it for:
    • Mirror to VRChat parameter, one per motor (named by its motor when the toy has several). It sends the motor's level (0 to 1) back to your avatar, for a glow or a fill meter. Type the name, or Pick… one your avatar sends.
    • Tell VRChat while this toy is connected, a Bool, on by default. See "It's connected" parameters.
  • Delete device, at the right end, forgets the toy and everything set up for it, in every mode. Switch it on again and it comes back as a new toy.

SPS Sources​

Your own zones, built from raw VRChat contact receivers: for spots the standard SPS zones don't cover, or an avatar without them. Each source is a zone you can pick in any toy's Input stage, like your avatar's own.

A source combines:

  • Proximity: one or more proximity receivers. The loudest wins.
  • Activation: on/off contacts. The proximity only counts while one of them fires. Leave it empty for no gate.
  • Velocity: on-enter contacts. While one fires, the level is multiplied by Velocity ×.
  • Max value: a cap on the proximity before the multiplier.

Type the contact names separated by commas. The /avatar/parameters/ prefix is optional. Sources are shared by every mode.

Tuning tools​

For when you shape how a toy responds:

  • the input simulator: a stroke to tune against, without a partner;
  • the signal graph of one chain.

The fold remembers whether you left it open.

Chimes and SteamVR​

  • A chime plays when a toy connects (going up) or disconnects (going down). Settings → Connection Settings → Sound when a toy connects or disconnects switches it off, with its volume beside it.
  • Show toys in SteamVR (Settings, on by default, Windows only) lists your connected toys in SteamVR's device strip with their battery. Just for show: they can never be picked up as trackers. Switching it off takes them out of SteamVR again. A PC without SteamVR is never touched.
