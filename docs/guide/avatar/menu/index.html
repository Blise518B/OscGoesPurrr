The avatar menu

OGP's controls can live in your avatar's own menu, so you never leave VRChat: strength, Off, Sleep, Test and the modes, a Friends submenu that drives whoever you picked in OGP, and the Avatar Remote's slots.

Settings → VRChat menu: the three parts of the menu, whether the avatar you wear has them, and the download.

Add it with VRCFury​

You need VRCFury in your avatar's Unity project (Creator Companion → Manage Project → add VRCFury).

  1. Download OscGoesPurrr-VRCFury.unitypackage (Settings → VRChat menu → Get the menu prefabs does the same) and import it into your avatar's Unity project.
  2. Drag one of its prefabs from the new Assets/OGP folder onto your avatar, anywhere inside it:
    • OscGoesPurrr Menu: everything, your own controls and the Friends menu, in one OscGoesPurrr submenu.
    • OscGoesPurrr Menu (Self): only your own controls (Avatar Remote included).
    • OscGoesPurrr Menu (Friends): only the Friends menu, as an OGP Friends submenu.
  3. Upload.

VRCFury adds the parameters and the submenu when you upload. The parameters are unsynced, so they cost 0 sync bits. Your avatar's own menu and parameter files aren't touched: to take it out again, delete the object.

The submenu lands in your root menu. To put it in another submenu, type that submenu's name into the Prefix field of the prefab's Full Controller.

A newer package imports over the old one and updates it in place.

No VRCFury? The setup guide has a one-click installer for Unity and the manual way.

Does my avatar have it?​

Settings → VRChat menu says, for the avatar you wear, which of the three parts it has: Your own controls, Friends submenu, Avatar Remote submenu. The Friends page says it too.

Your own controls​

ParameterTypeWhat it does
OGP/StrengthFloat 0–1Total output strength. Never touches your tuning.
OGP/ModeInt 0–3The active mode: 0 Combined, 1 Separate, 2 Custom 1, 3 Custom 2. Renaming a mode doesn't change its number.
OGP/OffBoolPanic silence. Off again and your level comes back.
OGP/SleepBoolToys wake only after three full strokes within six seconds.
OGP/TestBool, heldWhile held, every connected motor runs at about 20 %.

The menu and the app agree both ways: a change in OGP shows in your menu. After you load an avatar, OGP sends its own values; for about two seconds it ignores what VRChat replays then. So an avatar swap never changes your settings.

The Friends submenu​

It acts on whatever you picked on the Friends page: a friend, or a group. Each friend only gets what they allow you. To drive yourself and a friend at once, put yourself in a group with them (see Groups and Me).

  • Toys ▸: a level (OGP/Friend/Level), an auto mode (Sine, Square, Triangle, Saw, Random, Heartbeat, Build-up, Oscillate) with its speed and range, Override, which of their toys 1–4, and a stop.
  • Shockers ▸: Shock, Shocker vibrate and Beep buttons, strength and length as a part of their caps, PiShock's Small warning, which shockers 1–4, and a stop. A shock is a vibrate while they haven't armed.
  • Their strength: their total output strength slider. It moves on their screen too.

Every parameter is listed in the setup guide.

The Avatar Remote submenu​

Seven toggles and seven radials (OGP/Remote/Toggle1–7, OGP/Remote/Radial1–7) that you hand out to controls on the My Avatar Remote page. See Menu slots.

tip

Added the menu to an avatar you've used before, and nothing happens when you tap it? VRChat keeps an old list of the avatar's parameters. See Common problems.
