Common problems and how to solve them

Something not working? Find it below. Most of the time OGP already says what's wrong: rest the mouse on the thing that looks off, or press F1.

Install​

"Windows protected your PC"​

  • Cause: the exe isn't code-signed, so Windows doesn't know it yet.
  • Solution: click More info → Run anyway. Windows asks only the first time.

Toys​

My toy isn't found​

  • Cause: a toy talks to one thing at a time. If its phone app (or another program) has it, OGP can't. Or this PC's Bluetooth is off.
  • Solution: close the toy's own app, switch the toy off and on, and press Scan now on the Toys page. Check that Bluetooth is on. The sidebar's Intiface row should say CONNECTED.

OGP doesn't see the Intiface Central on my phone​

  • Cause: OGP still uses its built-in engine, or the phone doesn't let others in.
  • Solution: on the phone, switch on Listen on all network interfaces (and Broadcast Server Info via mDNS to be found without an address), and start the server. In OGP, Settings → Intiface Engine → switch off Use built-in Intiface engine, and type the phone's address if it isn't found. It needs Intiface Central 3.0 or newer. See Where your toys connect.

My toy doesn't react when I'm touched​

  • Cause: the toy doesn't listen to that contact: its zones don't include it, or its chips leave out that kind of touch. A touch from yourself is off for a new toy.
  • Solution: on Home, check the toy's four chips (Touch / Penetration from Self / Others). On the Toys page, open the toy's chain → Input → Select Zones, and pick All SPS or the zone. Make sure the toy isn't on Mute.

Everything is silent, and Off isn't on​

  • Cause: total output strength is at 0 (a menu radial parked at 0 looks exactly like this), you're AFK in VRChat, or Sleep is on.
  • Solution: check the strength slider. The status bar says AFK: everything off while you're away. Sleep waits for three full strokes.

Sleep seems dead​

  • Cause: that's Sleep working: nothing plays until three full strokes land within six seconds, so a brush can't wake it.
  • Solution: switch Sleep off for normal play.

Switching modes did nothing​

  • Cause: modes are routings, not strengths. If two modes route the same motors from the same zones, they feel the same.
  • Solution: set them apart on the Toys page → the toy → Input, with the mode you want to change active.

One toy is much stronger than the rest​

  • Cause: toys differ.
  • Solution: leave total strength alone. Trim that toy: Toys → the toy → its chain → Output → Gain. If it does nothing low down or is too much at the top, set Output → Range.

A toy keeps running after my partner left​

  • Cause: VRChat sent the contact's last value and then nothing more.
  • Solution: leave Anti-stuck on: the Toys page's, under Toy server, and the toy's own Anti-stuck chip on its bar. It stops a frozen value after 1 s, or eases off one held at 100 % after 10 s. The suits and the headset have their own on their pages.

VRChat and your avatar​

The VRChat row doesn't say CONNECTED​

  • Cause: OSC is off in VRChat, or VRChat isn't running yet.
  • Solution: in VRChat, Action menu → Options → OSC → Enabled. OGP finds VRChat by itself. OSC Diagnostics, under TOOLS in the sidebar, shows what OGP sees.

"Avatar has SPS" stays grey​

  • Cause: the avatar you're wearing has no SPS (or OGB / TPS) contacts, or VRChat hasn't told OGP about it: OSC is off, or VRChat keeps an old list of the avatar's parameters from before you added SPS.
  • Solution: wear an avatar with SPS sockets or plugs, with the VRChat row saying CONNECTED. Added SPS to an avatar you used before? Reset VRChat's list as in The avatar menu does nothing. An SPS 3 avatar needs nothing else: OGP switches its receivers on. No SPS on the avatar? Build your own zones under SPS Sources.

The avatar menu does nothing​

  • Cause: VRChat keeps an old list of each avatar's OSC parameters. An avatar you used before you added OGP's menu still has the old one.
  • Solution: close VRChat, delete the folder %USERPROFILE%\AppData\LocalLow\VRChat\VRChat\OSC\ (just that folder; VRChat makes it again), and load the avatar.

Every control shows twice in my menu​

  • Cause: the avatar has both the VRCFury prefab and the older one-click installer's submenu, and VRCFury merged them.
  • Solution: remove the "OscGoesPurrr" submenu the installer added to your expressions menu. Its parameters can stay.

My menu has a "Both of us" folder that does nothing​

  • Cause: it's from an older prefab. Driving yourself and a friend at once is a group with you in it now.
  • Solution: drag in the newer prefab. See Groups and Me.

The Height box is greyed out​

  • Cause: the world you're in forbids changing your height.
  • Solution: nothing to fix: it works again in a world that allows it.

Shockers​

My shocks arrive as vibrations​

  • Cause: shocks aren't armed. Until you arm them, every shock is a vibrate. The red words say Shocks not armed.
  • Solution: switch on Arm shocks (Home's shocker row or the Shockers page) and answer Yes. Read Arming and limits first.

A friend's Shock button is greyed​

  • Cause: the other side doesn't allow it: Shock is switched off on that shocker, or Vibrate is off there and they haven't armed (an unarmed shock goes out as a vibrate). The card says which.
  • Solution: ask them. It's their call.

The hub isn't found​

  • Cause: another program has the hub's port open (only one can), or the cable only charges.
  • Solution: close other shocker apps, try another USB cable, and press ↻ next to the port.

ShockOSC behaviours do nothing​

  • Cause: the avatar's ShockOSC settings say intensity 0, or Paused. They and the behaviours keep the same values, so the behaviours' strength is 0 or they're switched off.
  • Solution: turn the avatar's Intensity up or unpause it in its menu, or change the behaviours on the Shockers page: the avatar follows.

Friends​

A friend shows offline​

  • Cause: they're offline, or OGP hasn't looked for them yet. It looks in the background, gently.
  • Solution: press ↻ on their row, or ↻ Check all.

My second OGP window won't go online​

  • Cause: one OGP per PC goes online.
  • Solution: use the first window.

No friend can drive me​

  • Cause: Pause all is on; or Only friends in my instance is on while Read VRChat's log is off, so nobody is seen in your instance; or their switches are off.
  • Solution: check the top of the Friends page and the friend's row. See VRChat accounts and your instance.

A friend's panel says "Not here"​

  • Cause: they have Only friends in my instance on, and you're not in their VRChat instance (or they haven't linked you).
  • Solution: join them in VRChat, or ask them to link your account or allow you from anywhere.

The web remote link stopped working​

  • Cause: the link was replaced (New link…), switched off, or that browser was kicked.
  • Solution: ask for the new link. A kicked browser can't come back with the old one.

Suits and the headset​

My bHaptics suit won't connect​

  • Cause: the bHaptics Player is running. A suit takes one connection at a time.
  • Solution: close the bHaptics Player, then press Connect. Let go hands the suit back to the Player later.

PSVR2: Feel it is silent​

  • Cause: the headset isn't jailbroken (the commands go through, the motor never moves), or the toolkit isn't one of its experimental builds.
  • Solution: see PSVR2.

Still stuck?​

The System Log page (under TOOLS) has what OGP did and why. The issues on GitHub are the place to ask.
