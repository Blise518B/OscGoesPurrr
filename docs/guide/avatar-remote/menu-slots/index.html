Menu slots

The avatar menu has OscGoesPurrr ▸ Avatar Remote: seven Toggles and seven Radials. Each is a slot you give a control, or a whole category, on the My Avatar Remote page. Then you use your board from VRChat's own menu.

Give a control a slot​

  1. Click the small circle in a card's top bar (or a category's).
  2. A radial opens like VRChat's: Back at the top, 1–7 clockwise, each saying what has it now. Click a number: the control takes that slot, from whatever had it.

The circle then shows the slot's number. Click a number it already has to free it. A category can sit in both sets: the middle of the picker switches between Toggles and Radials. Back, Esc or a click elsewhere closes the picker.

  • Toggles take toggles and hold buttons. A hold button is held while the toggle is on.
  • Radials take sliders and steppers, across each one's own range.
  • A slot holds one thing; one thing can take several slots.
  • A category's slot drives every control in it that fits.
  • The Height box takes a Radial.

Slots are kept per preset. The menu drives the board of the avatar you're wearing, and it follows the board: a toggle is on while any of its controls is on, a radial sits where its first control is.

note

VRChat can't show the names (they're baked in when you upload), so the menu says Toggle 1–7 and Radial 1–7. The page shows what has each.

Your own menu ignores locks: they're for friends.

ParameterTypeWhat it does
OGP/Remote/Toggle1–7BoolOn / off for the slot's controls; a hold button is held while it's on.
OGP/Remote/Radial1–7Float 0–1The slot's sliders or steppers, across each one's own range.
