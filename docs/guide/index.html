OscGoesPurrr

All your VRChat haptics, in one app.

OscGoesPurrr (OGP) reads the contacts on your VRChat avatar and turns every touch into real-time output on your devices:

  • Bluetooth toys: Lovense, We-Vibe, Kiiroo and anything else Buttplug.io supports.
  • Shockers: PiShock and OpenShock, over the hub's USB cable.
  • Suits and more: bHaptics and OWO suits, SteamVR trackers with a motor, and the PlayStation VR2 headset's rumble.

Friends can drive all of it over the internet, from their own OGP or from a link in their browser. You decide what each of them may use.

It's one file for Windows, and an AppImage for Linux. The toy server is built in, and OGP finds VRChat and your toys by itself: no IPs, no ports.

Home: two toys, the shocker hub, both suits and the headset, each with its quick switches. The sidebar on the left has the modes, total strength, Off and Sleep.

What you need​

  • A Windows PC (10 or 11), or Linux. Bluetooth for your toys.
  • VRChat with OSC switched on.
  • An avatar with SPS contacts (VRCFury's sockets and plugs; older OGB and TPS setups work too). That's the standard toy setup.
  • Optional: VRCFury in your avatar's Unity project, for OGP's own menu.

How this guide is laid out​

  • Getting started: from download to your first buzz, step by step.
  • Toys: Home, the Toys page, modes and strength, where the toy server runs, and anti-stuck and Sleep.
  • Your avatar: OGP's menu in your avatar, the parameters OGP sends back, and SPS 3 avatars.
  • Shockers: the hub, the safety rules, behaviours and ShockOSC avatars.
  • Friends: adding friends, what each may do, driving them, groups, the web remote and VRChat accounts.
  • Avatar Remote, Suits and trackers, PSVR2, Records and Settings and tools.
  • Common problems: what goes wrong, why, and what to do.
tip

OGP explains itself too. Rest the mouse on anything in the app to see what it does, or press F1 on any page for its steps on top of the page.

Not in this edition​

DG-Lab Coyote e-stim and The Handy aren't in this edition yet. The roadmap on the website says what's coming.
