Statistics

How much your gear gets used, and when. Statistics stay on your PC and are never sent to anyone, friends included.

It's on by default (Settings → Features → Usage statistics). Switching it off stops recording and keeps what's recorded. Delete statistics…, right under the switch, deletes it all.

Statistics: every session with its length, active time, thrusts and a sparkline. The running one says recording.

Sessions​

Each session with its length, active time, thrusts, who you were with, and a sparkline. Show more lists older ones. Click a session for its page:

  • its timeline: each toy's intensity, thrusts per minute and which zones were touched;
  • a Shockers lane: a mark for every shock, vibrate and beep, as tall as it was strong. Hover for when, how strong, how long, which shocker and from whom;
  • its totals and an analysis: active time, thrusts, pace, your longest streak and your hottest five minutes, plus who drove you and what reached your shockers.

Lifetime​

Totals since you started, and Fun facts. Suits and trackers get their on-time next to your toys once one has buzzed.

When you play, Month​

A heatmap of the hours you play, and a month calendar. ← and → page through the months.

People​

Who your sessions were with, ranked by thrusts: sessions, active time, thrusts, peak pace, last time.

  • Tag a session with who it was with: the whole session, or a stretch you drag out on its timeline.
  • OGP can tag it for you while you're alone in an instance with one other person, from VRChat's own log. That's opt-in.
  • A friend linked to their VRChat account has one name in both places.

From whom​

Every shock, vibrate and beep that reached your shockers: how many, the strongest, and for how long. Lifetime or This session.

From whom: shocks, vibrates and beeps by person, with your avatar's behaviours, your menu and Party Mode as rows of their own, and per shocker.

  • By person: your avatar's behaviours one by one ("Touch zones · Neck"), what you sent yourself from your avatar menu or Party Mode, each friend and each web remote guest.
  • Per shocker.
  • How long friends drove your toys, and how many Avatar Remote controls they set.
  • Keep friends' names (on by default): off, new entries count as "A friend". Forget names… folds the names already kept.

Lifetime totals stay after the Log's 30 days.

Thrust counters on your avatar​

The same numbers can go to your avatar as Int parameters, for a counter display. See Thrust counters.
