PSVR2 headset rumble

The PlayStation VR2 headset has a rumble motor. OGP drives it from one contact on your avatar: a light touch is a slow thump, a full one a fast buzz.

Switch it on first: Settings → Features → PSVR2 — the headset's rumble, through PSVR2Toolkit, or tick A PlayStation VR2 headset in What are you here for?. It's off by default, and Windows only.

The PSVR2 page: the link to the toolkit, the rumble band, the contact that drives it, and Tell VRChat.

What you need​

  • PSVR2Toolkit, one of its experimental builds (the stable release doesn't have what OGP talks to). You install it yourself; OGP never bundles it. Run it once so OGP can find it.
  • A jailbroken headset. On a stock one the commands go through and the motor never moves.
  • SteamVR running with the toolkit.

Set it up​

  1. Start SteamVR with the toolkit. Link says when OGP is joined to it. Connect whenever SteamVR runs (on once the feature is on) does that by itself.
  2. Under Contact, press Pick… and choose the contact receiver on your avatar that drives it. A Haptic Pancake avatar has HapticHead.
  3. Press Feel it. Silent while the page says connected? The headset isn't jailbroken.
  4. Set the rumble band: Lowest and Highest. The motor does 10–25 Hz; 0 is off.

Safety​

  • Off, total output strength, VRChat going silent, Off while away and its own anti-stuck (at the bottom of the Contact card, with its own times) all hold it back.
  • If OGP stops sending, the motor stops within half a second.
  • On on Home's headset row (or Stop on this page) stops it.

Tell VRChat while the headset is connected sends OGP/PSVR2Connected.
