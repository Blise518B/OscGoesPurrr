How it works

From a touch in VR
to a buzz in the room

Your avatar is touched

The OGB / SPS contacts on your avatar notice every touch, stroke and thrust.

VRChat sends it over OSC

OscGoesPurrr finds VRChat on its own and reads your avatar's parameters. No IP or port typing, ever.

The signal chain shapes it

Depth, speed and punch are mixed, woken, smoothed and fitted to your toy — per contact, per toy.

The built-in Intiface engine sends it

It speaks Buttplug.io to your toys over Bluetooth. There's nothing else to install or run.

Your toy responds

Milliseconds later.

Features

Just works out of the box.
Every knob, if you want it.

📦

One file, zero setup

No installer, no Python, no separate Intiface Central. The exe carries its own Buttplug server.

📡

Finds everything itself

VRChat is found on your network automatically, toys the moment they're switched on.

🎚️

One bar for everything

Total output strength scales every toy at once — and never touches your tuning, so turning it back up brings back exactly the feel you had.

💤

Off and Sleep

Off is instant silence. Sleep only wakes for three real strokes in six seconds, so a brush against a sleeping partner does nothing.

🔀

Four routing modes

Combined, Separate and two custom modes decide which parts of your avatar drive which toys. Each avatar can remember its own.

🎮

Control it from VRChat

A strength radial plus Mode, Off, Sleep and Test in your avatar's menu. Unsynced, so they cost 0 sync bits.

💞

Touch and penetration, apart

Separate chains per kind of contact, each with its own feel — and a touch can go quiet while the pen is in.

🎯

Fitted to every toy

A gain and a min / max band per chain that remaps rather than clips. Strokers like the Solace Pro or an OSR2 follow depth.

🪞

Mirror to your avatar

Send any toy's output back to VRChat as a parameter to drive a glow, a blendshape or a meter — even with no toy connected.

🛡️

Never stuck on

OSC only sends changes, so a frozen contact could hold a motor on forever — after an avatar swap, or when your partner leaves. Anti-stuck timers cut it.

⏭️

Record, replay, simulate

Replay a recorded session or a built-in wave through your setup, and tune it with nobody else around.

📊

Stats and diagnostics

Active time, thrusts, per-toy and per-zone totals, a live OSC inspector — all kept on your PC.

?

It explains itself

No manual to read first and no help mode to switch on. Rest the pointer on any button, slider, toggle or heading and it tells you what it does — down to each knob of a signal chain. And when a new version is out, one click downloads it, checks it and restarts.

Questions

Good to know

Why does Windows warn me about it?
The exe isn't code-signed — a certificate costs a few hundred a year — so Windows SmartScreen doesn't recognise it yet. The whole source is public on GitHub and every release is built from it. Click More info → Run anyway.
Do I need Intiface Central?
No. OscGoesPurrr carries its own Intiface engine and starts it for you. If you'd rather keep using Intiface Central, switch off Use built-in Intiface engine in Settings and it connects to Central instead.
Which toys work?
Anything Buttplug.io supports: Lovense, We-Vibe, Kiiroo, Satisfyer, Magic Motion and many more, linear strokers included. The IoST Index lists them all.
Which avatars work?
Any avatar with OGB / SPS contacts — the standard sockets, plugs and touch zones toy apps use. They're picked up automatically when you load the avatar. For anything unusual, SPS Sources builds a zone out of your avatar's own contact receivers.
Does it work with a Quest?
Yes, as long as VRChat runs on your PC — over Link, Air Link or Virtual Desktop. OscGoesPurrr runs on Windows next to it.
Does it send anything over the internet?
Only one thing: at launch it asks GitHub whether there's a newer release, and you can switch that off in Settings. Everything else — VRChat, your toys, your statistics — stays on your PC and your local network.
Where are my settings?
In %APPDATA%\OscGoesPurrr\. A snapshot is taken at every launch (the newest five are kept) and can be restored from Settings.
What does it cost?
Nothing. It's free and open source under the MIT license.
Something isn't working — where do I get help?
Start with the app's own pages: OSC Diagnostics shows whether VRChat is reaching it and the System Log shows what happened. Still stuck? Open an issue on GitHub.
