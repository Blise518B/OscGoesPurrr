# The web remote

Someone without OGP can still drive you: share your **web remote** link.
Whoever opens it gets a friend's whole panel in their browser, the same as in
the app: your toys with every mode, your shockers as PiShock's panel with
Party Mode (or OpenShock's, with Live control, at their **Shocker buttons**
switch), and your avatar's board. No OGP, no account.

## Share it

1. On the Friends page, press **Copy web remote link (no OGP needed)**. It's
   right under **＋ Add a friend**.
2. Paste it anywhere. It's a plain link that opens in a browser, with a line
   under it saying whose toys it drives.

One link per OGP. Everyone on it shows under one **Web remote** row, right
after you in the list. Click that row for its panel.

## What they may do

On the Web remote's panel, **WHAT THEY MAY DO** is for everyone on the link at
once:

- **Toys**: on from the start. Levels, auto modes, Override and your strength
  slider.
- **Shockers** and **Avatar**: **off until you tick them**, because anyone
  with the link gets them.

Everything a friend's drive obeys, the web remote obeys too: Pause all, your
Off and strength, each toy's output band, arming and each shocker's limits,
locked categories, zero when its updates stop. It's all logged.

## Who's on

- **ON NOW** lists every browser on your link right now. **Kick** lets one go:
  it can't come back with your link.
- **On** switches the link off: nobody gets in, and everyone on it is let go.
- **New link…**: your link stops working and everyone on it is let go. Share
  the new one.
- **Remove web remote…** takes it away entirely.

A dead, replaced or switched-off link tells the browser so.

:::info Privacy
The web remote is never a friend or a contact, and it never hears your
friends' names: who else drives you reaches it only as a count. The page
always connects through n0's free relays; no server of ours is involved.
:::
