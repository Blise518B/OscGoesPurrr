# What each friend may do

You decide, friend by friend, what they may use. Everything they do is
logged.

## Three switches per friend

Every friend's row has three boxes, under the column icons:

| Column | Icon | Lets them |
|---|---|---|
| **Toys** | the pink capsule | drive your toys |
| **Shockers** | the yellow bolt | use your shockers |
| **Avatar** | the green remote | see and use your [Avatar Remote](../avatar-remote/friends.md) board |

All three are on for a new friend. A click on a column's icon sets that
column for every friend (off first, while any has it on).

Your shockers also need **Share with friends** on: it's the switch for all
friends at once, on Home and on the Shockers page. See
[how the switches stack](../shockers/safety.md#how-the-switches-stack).

## Pause all

**Pause all**, at the top of the Friends page, stops everything every friend
sends: toys, shockers, avatar buttons, your height. It covers the web remote
and your own Me row too. Switch it off and they can go on.

## Sync with others

**Sync friends**, next to Pause all, is yours alone to choose, for everyone
driving your toys: friends, the web remote and your own Me row. On (the
default), they share one set of toy cards: what one of them sets moves on
everyone's cards, and your toys play the newest change. Off, each drives on
their own, and your toys follow whichever of them is higher. Their panels
show **SYNCED** or **SEPARATE** and can't change it.

## Your shockers: the same limits for everyone

What a friend may do with your shockers is what each shocker allows on the
[Shockers page](../shockers/safety.md): its **Up to …** limits, its
**Shock**, **Vibrate** and **Beep** switches, whether it's on, arming, and
the rests. Every friend, every group, your own Me row and the web remote get
exactly those. There is nothing to set per friend: change a shocker's
limits on the Shockers page and it holds for all of them. The one split is
your avatar versus friends: with
[Separate limits for friends](../shockers/safety.md#separate-limits-for-friends)
on, all friends together get their own caps and timing.

Their panel shows those limits: the sliders stop there, and what a shocker
doesn't allow is greyed, with why.

## What always holds

Whatever a friend may do, these stay yours:

- **Your toys**: a friend's level stays under your total output strength,
  your Off, and each toy's output band. It drops to zero when their updates
  stop.
- **Your shockers**: every shock goes through the same gate as your own:
  [arming, limits and the rest](../shockers/safety.md). A friend can't widen
  any of them.
- **Your avatar**: a locked category is look-only for friends.
- **Everything is logged**, on the Friends page and the
  [Log](../records/log.md).

[Only friends in your instance](./vrchat-accounts.md#only-friends-in-your-instance).
