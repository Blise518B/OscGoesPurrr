# Friends and your board

Friends whose **Avatar** box is ticked in your friend list see the board of
the avatar you're wearing, under your toys on their Friends page, and can use
it. Everything they do is logged.

- They see your **worn** avatar's active preset, never one you opened for
  offline editing.
- A **locked** category (🔒) is look-only for them.
- **Friends looking**, at the top of My Avatar Remote, shows who has your
  board open right now.
- Nothing is sent to a friend until they have your board open. Then only
  changes.

## Their board

On a friend's panel, **THEIR AVATAR** is their worn avatar's board, if they
use the Avatar Remote and let you. Press a toggle, hold a button, move a
slider. A locked category says **look only**.

## ⚡ YOLO

**⚡ YOLO** hands every friend you allow *every* parameter of the avatar you
wear, not only your board (VRChat's own, like Viseme, aside). Locks don't
hold for them then. It asks before it goes on, and stays on until you switch
it off. One switch for everyone.

## Stopping them

- Untick a friend's **Avatar** box: they lose your board.
- **Pause all** on the Friends page blocks every set, and lets go of any
  button a friend holds.
- A button a friend holds is let go when their updates stop.

The [web remote](../friends/web-remote.md) gets your board only once you tick
its **Avatar** switch.
