# Getting started

From download to your first buzz. Each step takes a minute.

## 1. Download and run OscGoesPurrr

- Download
  [OscGoesPurrr-Windows.exe](https://github.com/Blise518B/OscGoesPurrr/releases/latest/download/OscGoesPurrr-Windows.exe)
  and run it. It's one file: nothing to install, Intiface is built in.
- Windows shows a blue *"Windows protected your PC"* box the first time,
  because the exe isn't code-signed. Click **More info → Run anyway**.
- On Linux, get the AppImage instead: see [Linux](./help/linux.md).

New versions install themselves. OGP tells you when there's one.

## 2. Tick what you'll use

The first launch asks **What are you here for?** Tick what you have and what
you want:

- **What you have**: Toys, A shocker, A bHaptics suit or sleeves, An OWO
  suit, Trackers that vibrate, A PlayStation VR2 headset.
- **What you want**: Play with friends over the internet, An avatar remote
  friends can use too, Statistics of my sessions, OSC Inspector (for avatar
  makers).

Press **Done**. OGP shows the pages for what you ticked and hides the rest.
**Skip** changes nothing. You can change it any time in
[Settings → Features](./settings/features.md).

Then the **guided tour** starts. It lights up one control at a time and waits
for you to do the real thing. Follow it, or press **End tour** and use these
steps.

## 3. Connect your toy

- Switch your toy on. OGP's built-in engine finds it over Bluetooth by itself.
- Close the toy's own phone app first. A toy talks to one thing at a time.
- No toy yet? The [Toys page](./toys/toys-page.md) has **Scan now**, which
  looks right away.

Your toys paired with **Intiface Central on a phone** (or another PC)? That
works too: see [Where your toys connect](./toys/toy-server.md).

## 4. Turn on OSC in VRChat

- In VRChat: **Action menu → Options → OSC → Enabled**.
- OGP finds VRChat by itself. The sidebar's **VRChat** row says
  **CONNECTED** once it does.

## 5. Set up your avatar

Your avatar needs **SPS** contacts: VRCFury's sockets and plugs, the standard
toy setup. Older OGB and TPS avatars work too.

Until everything works, Home's **Getting started** line shows where you are:
**Toy on · VRChat connected · Avatar has SPS · Avatar menu**. A dot turns
green when that part works, and the line goes away once the first three are
green. The avatar menu is optional.

**Optional:** drag OGP's VRCFury prefab onto your avatar, so strength, Off,
Sleep and the modes are in your avatar's own menu. See
[The avatar menu](./avatar/menu.md).

## 6. Choose what each toy feels

1. On **Home**, click your toy's row. It opens on the Toys page.
2. Click **Input**, then **Select Zones**: these are your SPS sources.
   **All SPS** hears every zone your avatar has. Or switch on only the ones you
   want.
3. Back on Home, the four chips on the toy's row choose who it feels:
   **Touch** from **Self** or **Others**, **Penetration** from **Self** or
   **Others**.

A new toy hears touch and penetration from others.

![One toy's row on Home](./toys/img/home-toy-row.png)
*A toy's row on Home: what drives it (Ring), the four chips, its battery, a live meter per motor, Mute and Test.*

## 7. Test it

- Press **Test all** on Home. Every connected toy buzzes for about a second.
- **Total output strength** in the sidebar turns everything up or down. It
  never touches your tuning.
- **Off** in the sidebar silences everything at once.

If you feel it, you're set.

## 8. You're done!

Have fun. When you want more:

- Tune how a toy feels: [The Toys page](./toys/toys-page.md).
- Control OGP from VRChat: [The avatar menu](./avatar/menu.md).
- A shocker: [The hub](./shockers/hub.md), and read
  [Arming and limits](./shockers/safety.md) first.
- Friends: [Adding friends](./friends/adding.md).
- Something not working? [Common problems](./help/common-problems.md).
