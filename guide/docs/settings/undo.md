# Undo and redo

Changed something you didn't mean to? <kbd>Ctrl</kbd>+<kbd>Z</kbd> takes it
back, wherever you are in OGP. <kbd>Ctrl</kbd>+<kbd>Y</kbd> steps forward
again.

- OGP remembers your last **five** changes, for the whole window.
- It covers every setting: toys' chains and modes, the Shockers page, Avatar
  Remote boards, the Friends list and groups, Settings.
- The status bar says what it did, for example **Undid: removed Ears from the
  Avatar Remote**.
- A new change after an undo drops what you could still redo.

## Only settings

Undo takes back settings, never what already happened: a shock, a toy's
level, a friend's drive, a message sent. Changes made by a friend, your
avatar's menu or OGP itself aren't steps either. Only what you changed.

## Safety still asks

An undo that would **arm your shockers**, switch a shocker or one of its
actions back on, widen what a friend may do, or raise a limit asks first, the
same way doing it by hand does. See [Arming and limits](../shockers/safety.md).
